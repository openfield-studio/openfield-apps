Privacy Policy – TideChart (作業潮位表)

Last updated: August 23, 2026

TideChart / 作業潮位表 ("the App") is a tide-level reference application developed by OpenField Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

TideChart does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or with any third party. Specifically:

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • No personal information (name, email address, etc.) is collected.
  • No usage analytics or crash reports are sent to external servers.
  • The App does not use advertising networks (no AdMob or third-party ads).
  • The App does not send any of your data to our servers — all tide calculations run entirely on your device.

2. Location Information

The App can optionally use your device's location to find the nearest tide station ("最寄りの検潮所" button). This calculation happens entirely on your device: your coordinates are compared against a built-in list of tide stations, and the result is used only to preselect a station in the App's own list. Your location is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and is not stored after the calculation completes.

You can decline the location permission and still use the full App by selecting your region, prefecture, and station manually.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data created or selected while using the App is stored exclusively on your device (using the browser's localStorage mechanism and/or Android's local app storage) and is never transmitted externally. This includes:

  • Your selected region / prefecture / tide station.
  • Custom points you save ("マイ地点") and their reference settings.
  • Alarm/notification settings you configure.
  • The date your free trial started, used only to determine whether the trial period is still active.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Subscription (In-App Purchases)

TideChart offers an optional monthly subscription after a free trial period. All purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, see, or store your payment card details or any other billing information — Google handles this on our behalf. The App only receives a subscription status (active / not active) from Google Play, which is used locally to determine whether the App's features are unlocked.

Purchases are subject to Google Play's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Permissions

TideChart requests the following device permissions:

  • Location (optional) — used only as described in Section 2, entirely on-device.
  • Billing — required by Google Play to process the optional subscription described in Section 4.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other device permission.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
